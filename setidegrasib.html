Global Net Sales Forecast ($M)

Starting forecast assumptions: Global sales are $0 through 2030. Illustrative launches begin in 2032, with 2035 global sales of $1,000M in first-line metastatic PDAC and $650M in previously treated KRAS G12D NSCLC; these levels are held flat through 2040 as placeholders. Sales ramps and a 45% U.S. share are modeling estimates, not published guidance. The 2040 horizon is a provisional LOE planning placeholder, not a verified Setidegrasib patent or regulatory exclusivity date; post-LOE erosion is not modeled. R&D is held at $40M annually from 2035 through 2040 as an editable continuation placeholder. COGS (25%), SG&A (28%), tax (21%), capex (1%), working capital (5%), and zero royalties are editable placeholders; zero royalties means no undisclosed licensing obligation is modeled, not that none exists. R&D is editable in the forecast table and defaults to estimated program spending, not Astellas-reported asset-level expense. Milestones default to zero because no external deal terms have been incorporated.

Risk adjustment: The PoS row is now calculated from the selected current phase and the editable cumulative probability of approval from that phase. Both indications default to Phase III. The defaults (Phase I 5.3%, Phase II 10.8%, Phase III 43.9%, NDA/BLA 92.0%) are historical oncology benchmarks from the BIO/QLS/Informa 2011–2020 study, not Setidegrasib-specific estimates. The model applies the probability to positive cash flows from the assumed launch year; negative development cash flows are counted in full. Thus rNPV can remain negative when the present value of development spending exceeds probability-adjusted commercial cash flows. BIO report and methodology.

How the math works: Eligible KRAS G12D+ patients = patients entering the setting × prevalence × testing rate × clinical eligibility. New starts = eligible patients × uptake. Starts are assumed to occur evenly through each year; average treatment duration carries cohorts forward into later years, including across the extended forecast. Sales = patient-months on Setidegrasib × net price per patient-month. The net price is an editable scenario input, not an observed or approved price. Do not include chemotherapy revenue in the Setidegrasib forecast.

LOE and extended-horizon assumption: The supplied market overviews do not identify Setidegrasib’s patent or regulatory exclusivity expiration. This calculator therefore uses 2040 as a provisional LOE planning placeholder, not a verified date. Forecasts stop in 2040 and do not model post-LOE generic/biosimilar erosion; update this year when patent and exclusivity diligence is available.

Seeded U.S. base case: The PDAC population grows 1% annually through 2040; the NSCLC 2L pool is reduced gradually to 30% below its 2026 level by 2034, then held flat. Uptake is zero before the assumed 2030 launch, then ramps to the market report’s class uptake × asset share (PDAC: 75% × 15% = 11.25%; NSCLC: 75% × 25% = 18.75%) by 2034. Treatment duration is 6.3 effective months (7 months × 90% compliance). Monthly net prices are illustrative: PDAC $31,200 (WAC $40,000 less 22% gross-to-net) and NSCLC $23,400 (WAC $30,000 less 22%). These are scenario inputs for a hypothetical class entrant, not Setidegrasib-specific guidance or an approved price. Clinical eligibility remains 100%; the market report captures fitness and class-choice limits in uptake, so this avoids counting them twice. The NPV tab holds 2035 sales flat through 2040 and carries R&D at $40M/year after 2035 as editable placeholders.

Reference inputs: The supplied market overviews use KRAS G12D prevalence of approximately 40% in PDAC and 4% in NSCLC; both prevalence figures are editable. The PDAC Phase 3 study is in first-line metastatic disease and the NSCLC Phase 3 study is in previously treated locally advanced or metastatic KRAS G12D disease. Population, testing, launch, uptake, treatment-duration, and price inputs are market-model assumptions; price and uptake are not Setidegrasib-specific. Astellas trial sources: PDAC study; NSCLC study; mutation prevalence source.
