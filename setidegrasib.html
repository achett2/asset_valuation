Global Net Sales Forecast ($M)

Starting forecast assumptions: Global sales are $0 through 2030. Illustrative launches begin in 2032, with 2035 global sales of $1,000M in first-line metastatic PDAC and $650M in previously treated KRAS G12D NSCLC. Sales ramps and a 45% U.S. share are modeling estimates, not published guidance. COGS (25%), SG&A (28%), tax (21%), capex (1%), working capital (5%), and zero royalties are editable placeholders; zero royalties means no undisclosed licensing obligation is modeled, not that none exists. R&D is editable in the forecast table and defaults to estimated program spending, not Astellas-reported asset-level expense. Milestones default to zero because no external deal terms have been incorporated.

Risk adjustment: NPV discounts each year’s forecast cash flow. rNPV leaves negative cash flows (including development investment) unweighted and applies each year’s cumulative PoS to positive cash flows only. Cumulative PoS is initially set at 35% for 2032 onward as a simplified combined-indication approval assumption; adjust it as clinical evidence changes. A decision-grade rNPV would model the indications separately and probability-adjust each revenue and cost stream based on decision timing.

Clinical context and sources:

  • Astellas Phase 3 NSCLC study announcement (first-line dosing announced September 2026; compares setidegrasib with docetaxel in previously treated NSCLC).
  • Astellas Phase 3 PDAC study announcement (first-line metastatic PDAC in combination with chemotherapy).
  • Phase 1 results in the New England Journal of Medicine: early antitumor activity supports development, but does not establish Phase 3 success or commercial uptake.
